Rent Platform — Build in Progress

A verified rental marketplace with an inspection/guarantee layer, immutable records, and PSP-facilitated payments. Bush-hiring a small tool: the platform instructs, the PSP executes, and the bot never approves money.

Never holds customer funds Webhook-driven & idempotent Immutable audit log

Static preview served locally on port 3000.

Identity & KYC

Phone OTP signup and tiered verification (T1 phone+ID, T2 +address, T3 +title doc), reviewed by humans.

Listings & Inspection

DRAFT → PENDING_INSPECTION → VERIFIED → LIVE, with geotagged media and a fixed re-inspection cadence.

Ledger & Receipts

An append-only ledger with receipts, cash recording confirmed or disputed by the tenant, and anomaly flags.

Payments via PSP

MTN MoMo and Orange Money behind one interface, with a fallback slot, retries, and reconciliation jobs.

Ground rules

  • The bot communicates; the rules engine decides; humans judge.
  • No tenant is locked out because of their payment method.
  • Every financial or state change writes an audit entry.
  • Boring, well-tested libraries over novel ones.

Status

Phase 0 foundations: repo scaffold, static deployment pipeline, and scripted desktop/mobile capture are wired up. Core phases land incrementally with tests passing before each phase ships.
